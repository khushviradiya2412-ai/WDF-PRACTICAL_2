FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions Pages

Questions students commonly ask about the Student Hub Portal.

1. How do I create a new account on the Student Hub Portal?

Go to the sign-up page, fill in your personal details, choose a secure password, and verify your email address to activate your account.

2. What can I do once I am logged into the portal?

You can view course materials, access announcements, submit assignments, check grades, and connect with faculty and classmates.

3. How do I reset my password if I forget it?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page, enter your registered email, and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.

4. Can I update my profile information later?

Yes. After logging in, go to your profile settings to change your contact details, profile picture, or other account information.

5. Where can I find help if I have trouble using the portal?

Visit the support section on the portal, contact the help desk, or use the built-in chat feature to get assistance from the student support team.
